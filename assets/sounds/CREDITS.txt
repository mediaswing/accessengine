Sound effects
=============

All three cues come from freesound.org and are released under Creative Commons
Zero 1.0 (CC0) — a dedication to the public domain, which asks for nothing in
return. They are credited here anyway: CC0 waives the requirement, not the
courtesy, and someone who wants more sounds that fit these ought to be able to
find where they came from.

success.wav
    "Success" by kagateni — https://freesound.org/s/404358/
    Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal

error.wav
    "Failure/Wrong action" by gabrielaraujo — https://freesound.org/s/242503/
    Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal

progress.wav
    "Digital" by nbs_dark — https://freesound.org/s/91898/
    Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal

tick.wav
    The same recording as progress.wav, and deliberately so: the tick means
    "the thing that started is still going", so it ought to sound like the
    thing that started. It is 180 ms taken from the middle of it, and it is
    the one file here that is *not* levelled with the others — see below.

Every file here is edited rather than original. Each was converted to mono,
trimmed of its leading and trailing silence — the success recording began with
two thirds of a second of nothing, which is an age when the sound is meant to
be the answer to a keypress — given a 5 ms fade in and a 30 ms fade out so
neither end clicks, and levelled to the same RMS loudness. That last step is
the one that matters most: as published, the failure sound was around 11 dB
quieter than the success one, so the sound telling you something went wrong
would have been the one you struggled to hear. All three announcement sounds
now sit at a mean of -22.2 dB.

tick.wav is the exception, at a mean of -33.6 dB — about 11 dB below the rest.
That is the same gap that was a bug between success and error, and here it is
the point: the other three each report something that has happened, and are
levelled to be heard. The tick reports that nothing has happened yet, and it
may sound a hundred times in one job, so it is mixed to sit under the app
rather than announce itself.
